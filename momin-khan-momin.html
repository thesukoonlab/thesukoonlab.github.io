THE POET

An intimate language
of longing.

Momin Khan Momin is remembered for the delicacy, emotional precision and romantic intensity of his ghazals. His poetry often finds extraordinary depth in a single glance, pause or turn of feeling.

The music of his language carries tenderness and ache together, making private emotion feel both precise and universal.
