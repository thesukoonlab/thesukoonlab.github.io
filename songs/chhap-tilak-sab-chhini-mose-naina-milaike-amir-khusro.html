Poetry & lyrics

The verses below follow the lyric text included in The Sukoon Lab's original release description. These traditional kalam are attributed here to Amir Khusrau.

Apni chhavi banay ke jo main pi ke paas gayi
Jab chhavi dekhi peehu ki to apni bhool gayi

Mose nainaan milay ke

Chhap tilak sab chheeni re mose nainaan milay ke
Baat adham keh deenhi re

Mose nainaan milay ke

Bal bal jaaun main tore rang-rezwa
Apni si rang deenhi re

Mose nainaan milay ke

Prem-bhati ka madwaa pilaay ke matwaari kar deenhi re
Matwaari kar deenhi re

Mose nainaan milay ke

Baiyaan pakar dhar leenhi re mose nainaan milay ke

Mose nainaan milay ke

Khusrau Nizaam ke bal bal jaiye
Mohe suhaagan keenhi re mose nainaan milay ke

Mose nainaan milay ke

Poetry: Amir Khusrau. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
