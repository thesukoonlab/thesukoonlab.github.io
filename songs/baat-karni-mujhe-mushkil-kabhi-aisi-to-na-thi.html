Poetry & lyrics

The original release description attributes these lyrics to Bahadur Shah Zafar. The text below follows that supplied source.

Baat karni mujhe mushkil kabhi aisi to na thi
Jaisi ab hai teri mehfil kabhi aisi to na thi

Le gaya chheen ke kaun aaj tera sabr-o-qaraar
Be-qaraari tujhe ai dil kabhi aisi to na thi

Us ki aankhon ne Khuda jaane kiya kya jaadu
Ki tabee'at meri maayil kabhi aisi to na thi

Ab ki jo raah-e-mohabbat mein uthaai takleef
Sakht hoti hamein manzil kabhi aisi to na thi

Nigaah-e-yaar ko ab kyun hai tagaful ai dil
Vo tere haal se ghaafil kabhi aisi to na thi

Chashm-e-qaatil meri dushman thi hamesha lekin
Jaisi ab ho gayi qaatil kabhi aisi to na thi

Kya sabab tu jo bigadta hai 'Zafar' se har baar
Khu teri hoor-shamaail kabhi aisi to na thi

Poetry: Bahadur Shah Zafar. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
