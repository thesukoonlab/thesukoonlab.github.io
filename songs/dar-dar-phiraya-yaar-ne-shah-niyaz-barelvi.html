Poetry & lyrics

The following Roman Urdu verses follow the lyric text included in The Sukoon Lab's original release description.

Hum ko yahan dar dar phiraya yaar ne
La makan mein ghar banaya yaar ne

Aap apne dekhne ke vaste
Ham ko aaina banaya yaar ne

Apne ik adna tamashe ke liye
Ham ko sooli par chadhaya yaar ne

Aap chhup ke ham ko kar ke rubaru
Khoob hi ruswa karaya yaar ne

Aap to but ban ke ki jalwa gari
Aur hamein kafir banaya yaar ne

Poetry: Shah Niyaz Barelvi. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
