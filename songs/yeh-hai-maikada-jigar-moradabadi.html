Yeh Hai Maikada / Jigar Moradabadi

Lyrics from this rendition

The following text is reproduced from the lyrics included in The Sukoon Lab's original release description.

Yeh hai maikada yahan rind hain
Yahan sab ka saqi imam hai
Ye haram nahi hai ai shaikh ji
Yahan parsai haram hai

Jo zara si pi ke bahak gaya
Use maikade se nikaal do
Yahan tang-nazar ka guzar nahi
Yahan ahal-e-zarf ka kaam hai

Koi mast hai koi tishna-lab
To kisi ke haath mein jaam hai
Magar is pe koi kare bhi kya
Ye to maikade ka nizam hai

Ye janab-e-shaikh ka falsafa hai
Ajeeb sare jahan se
Jo wahan piyo to halal hai
Jo yahan piyo to haram hai

Tujhe apne husn ka wasta
Mere shauq-e-deed pe raham kha
Zara muskura ke naqab utha
Ki nazar ko shauq-e-salam hai

Na to e'tikaf se kuch gharaz
Na sawab-o-zuhad se wasta
Tiri deed aisi namaz hai
Na sujud hai na qiyam hai

Mira farz hai ki pada rahoon
Tiri bargah mein saqiya
Koi tishna-lab hai ki sair hai
Yahi dekhna tira kaam hai

Isi kaayinat mein ai 'Jigar'
Koi inqilab uthega phir
Ki buland ho ke bhi aadmi
Abhi khwahishon ka ghulam hai

Poetry: Jigar Moradabadi. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
