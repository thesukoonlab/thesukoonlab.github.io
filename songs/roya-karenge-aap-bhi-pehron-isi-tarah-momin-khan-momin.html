Poetry & lyrics

The verses performed in this rendition are classical poetry by Momin Khan Momin. The text below follows the lyrics included in The Sukoon Lab's original release description.

Roya karenge aap bhi pehron isi tarah
Atka kahin jo aap ka dil bhi meri tarah

Aata nahin hai woh to kisi dhab se daaw mein
Banti nahin hai milne ki us ke koi tarah

Tashbeeh kis se doon ki tarah-daar ki mire
Sab se niraali waz'a hai sab se nai tarah

Na taab hijr mein hai na aaraam wasl mein
Kambakht dil ko chain nahin hai kisi tarah

Lagti hain gaaliyaan bhi tere munh se kya bhali
Qurbaan tere phir mujhe keh le usi tarah

Ma'shuq aur bhi hain bata de jahaan mein
Karta hai kaun zulm kisi par teri tarah

Hoon jaan-ba-lab butaan-e-sitamgar ke haath se
Kya sab jahaan mein jeete hain 'Momin' isi tarah

Poetry: Momin Khan Momin. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
