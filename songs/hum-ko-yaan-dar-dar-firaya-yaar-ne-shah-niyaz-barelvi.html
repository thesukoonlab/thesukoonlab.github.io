Poetry & lyrics

The verses below follow the lyric text included in The Sukoon Lab's original release description. These traditional kalam are attributed here to Shah Niyaz Barelvi.

Hum ko yaan dar dar phiraya yaar ne
La makan mein ghar banaya yaar ne

Aap apne dekhne ke waaste
Hum ko aaina banaya yaar ne

Apne ek adna tamashe ke liye
Hum ko sooli par charhaya yaar ne

Aap chhup ke hum ko kar ke rubaru
Khoob hi ruswa karaya yaar ne

Aap to but ban ke ki jalwagari
Aur hamein kafir banaya yaar ne

Poetry: Shah Niyaz Barelvi. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
