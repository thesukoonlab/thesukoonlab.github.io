Lyrics

The lyrics below follow the text included in The Sukoon Lab's original release description.

Saare jahan se achchha Hindostan hamara
Hum bulbulen hain is ki ye gulistan hamara

Gurbat mein hon agar hum rehta hai dil watan mein
Samjho wahin hamein bhi dil ho jahan hamara

Parbat wo sab se ooncha hum-saaya aasmaan ka
Wo santari hamara wo paasbaan hamara

Godi mein khelti hain is ki hazaaron nadiyan
Gulshan hai jin ke dam se rashk-e-jinaan hamara

Ae aab-e-Rood-e-Ganga wo din hai yaad tujh ko
Utra tere kinaare jab kaarvaan hamara

Mazhab nahin sikhata aapas mein bair rakhna
Hindi hain hum watan hai Hindostan hamara

Yunaan-o-Misr-o-Rooma sab mit gaye jahan se
Ab tak magar hai baaqi naam-o-nishaan hamara

Kuch baat hai ki hasti mitti nahin hamari
Sadiyon raha hai dushman daur-e-zamaan hamara

Iqbal koi mahram apna nahin jahan mein
Maaloom kya kisi ko dard-e-nihaan hamara

Saare jahan se achchha Hindostan hamara
Hum bulbulen hain is ki
Ye gulistan hamara

Poetry: Allama Iqbal. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
