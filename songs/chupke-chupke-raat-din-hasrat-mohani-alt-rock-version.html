Chupke Chupke Raat Din / Hasrat Mohani / Alt-Rock Version

Chupke Chupke Raat Din / Hasrat Mohani / Alt-Rock Version is part of The Sukoon Lab's collection of poetry-led music. This rendition connects the writing of Hasrat Mohani with a contemporary sound, while keeping the poet and the words at the centre of the experience.

Selected lyrics from this rendition

The original release description contains selected verses with ellipses indicating omitted passages. This section preserves that source text as an excerpt, not as a complete or unabridged ghazal.

Chupke chupke raat din...

Ansoon bahana yaad hai
Hum ko ab tak aashiqi ka wo zamana yaad hai

Tujh se kuch milte hi...

Wo bebaak ho jaana mira
Aur tira daanton mein wo ungli dabana yaad hai

Khench lena wo mira...

Parde ka kona dafatan
Aur dupatte se tira wo munh chupana yaad hai
Hum ko ab tak aashiqi ka wo zamana yaad hai

Jab siwa mere tumhara...

Koi deewana na tha
Sach kaho kuch tum ko bhi wo kar-khana yaad hai

Ghair ki nazron se bach kar...

Sab ki marzi ke khilaf
Wo tira chori-chupe raaton ko aana yaad hai
Hum ko ab tak aashiqi ka wo zamana yaad hai

Aa gaya gar wasl ki shab bhi...

Kahin zikr-e-firaq
Wo tira ro ro ke mujh ko bhi rulana yaad hai

Dopahar ki dhoop mein...

Mere bulane ke liye
Wo tira kothe pe nange paon aana yaad hai
Hum ko ab tak aashiqi ka wo zamana yaad hai

Poetry: Hasrat Mohani. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
