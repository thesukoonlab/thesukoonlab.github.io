Poetry & lyrics

The verses performed in this rendition are classical poetry by Mirza Ghalib. The text below follows the lyrics included in The Sukoon Lab's original release description.

Dil-e-naadaan tujhe hua kya hai
Aakhir is dard ki dawa kya hai

Hum hain mushtaaq aur woh bezaar
Ya ilahi yeh maajra kya hai

Main bhi munh mein zabaan rakhta hoon
Kaash poochho ke mudda kya hai

Jab ke tujh bin nahin koi maujood
Phir yeh hungama ai khuda kya hai

Yeh pari chehra log kaise hain
Ghamza o ishwa o ada kya hai

Hum ko unse wafa ki hai umeed
Jo nahin jaante wafa kya hai

Poetry: Mirza Ghalib. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
