Ye Ishq Nahi Asaan (Live Concert) / Jigar Moradabadi

Ye Ishq Nahi Asaan (Live Concert) / Jigar Moradabadi is part of The Sukoon Lab's collection of poetry-led music. This rendition connects the writing of Jigar Moradabadi with a contemporary sound, while keeping the poet and the words at the centre of the experience.

Lyrics

The verses below follow the lyrics included in The Sukoon Lab's original release description for this live-concert rendition.

Ye ishq nahin aasaan, bas itna samajh lijiye
Ik aag ka dariya hai, aur doob ke jaana hai

Ik lafz-e-mohabbat ka adna yeh fasana hai
Simte to dil-e-aashiq, phaile to zamaana hai

Hum ishq ke maaron ka itna hi fasana hai
Rone ko nahin koi, hansne ko zamaana hai

Aansoo to bahut se hain Jigar lekin
Bandh jaaye so moti hai, beh jaaye so paani hai

Poetry: Jigar Moradabadi. Musical arrangement, performance and sound recording: The Sukoon Lab.
