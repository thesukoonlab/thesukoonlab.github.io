THE POET

A bridge between worlds —
poetry, music and spirit.

Amir Khusrau was a poet, musician and mystic whose work became a bridge between Persian and Hindavi traditions. His poetry moves between earthly love and spiritual devotion, celebrating beauty, music and the search for union.

His legacy lives in qawwali, Sufi traditions and the shared cultural language of the subcontinent. His words continue to find new voices because they hold tenderness, devotion, joy and longing in the same breath.
