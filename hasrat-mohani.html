THE POET

A life of conviction
and tenderness.

Hasrat Mohani brought together romantic lyricism and political conviction. His poetry and public life were shaped by a commitment to freedom, making tenderness and resistance part of the same story.

His work reminds us that longing can be personal and collective: a desire for a beloved, for dignity and for a world with room to breathe.
