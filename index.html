03 / THE PRINCIPLE

THE WORDS ARE NOT OURS TO REWRITE.

The music can change.
The poetry remains.

The Sukoon Lab exists to bring original Urdu poetry to new listeners. Genres can shift, arrangements can transform and new musical worlds can be explored — but the poet's words remain intact.

If a couplet cannot be set to music without changing it, the choice is to leave the whole couplet out, not rewrite the poet to fit the arrangement.

The music is AI-produced under human creative direction. The poetry and the artistic choices that shape each interpretation remain at the heart of the project.
