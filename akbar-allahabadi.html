THE POET

A wit that held
up a mirror.

Akbar Allahabadi made satire a distinctive instrument of Urdu poetry. He observed changing society, modernity and public pretence with wit that could entertain while quietly unsettling the reader.

His work reminds us that poetry can be playful and incisive at once, finding a memorable line where a lecture would fail.
