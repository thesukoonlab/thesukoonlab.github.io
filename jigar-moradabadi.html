THE POET

A heart that sang
without restraint.

Jigar Moradabadi brought romantic intensity and musicality to the Urdu ghazal. His poetry turns desire, beauty and separation into language that seems made to be sung.

The emotional clarity of his work gives every melody room to breathe: intimate, passionate and unmistakably human.
