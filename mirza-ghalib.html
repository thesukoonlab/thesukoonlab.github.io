THE POET

A mind that questioned
everything.

Mirza Ghalib remains one of the most influential voices in Urdu poetry. His work moves between wit and wisdom, love and loss, scepticism and faith.

His couplets continue to speak to new generations because they hold contradiction, humour, heartbreak and a lifetime of thought.
