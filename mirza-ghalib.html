Mirza Ghalib

and philosophy.

THE POET

A mind that questioned
everything.

Mirza Ghalib remains one of the most influential voices in Urdu poetry. His work moves effortlessly between wit and wisdom, love and loss, scepticism and faith, reflecting a mind that questioned, observed and felt deeply.

His poetry continues to speak to new generations because of its honesty, complexity and humanity. A single couplet can hold contradiction, humour, heartbreak and a lifetime of thought.
